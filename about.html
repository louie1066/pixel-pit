About Pixel Pit

Pixel Pit is a free collection of retro-style arcade games, all made from scratch for this site. Each game takes inspiration from a classic style of play, but the characters, sprites, levels and sounds are original.

Every game shows how often it has been played. Each game also has a shared top 5 high score board that everyone sees, on any device.

Games are always dark. You can switch this site between light and dark mode, but each game keeps its dark look, so the graphics and colours work as designed.

Who runs the site

Pixel Pit is made by a computing teacher. Questions or feedback are welcome on the contact page.
