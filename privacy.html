Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

  • No accounts: You do not need to sign up or give your name to play.
  • High scores: If your score makes the top 5, we store the score, the date and the initials you enter.
  • Play counts: We count how many times each game is played. These counts are not linked to you.
  • Settings on your device: This site does not store anything on your device. Your light or dark mode choice lasts only while the page is open.
  • Messages: The contact form opens your own email app with your message ready to send; nothing is sent through this site. If you email us, we keep your address and message only so we can reply.

Cookies and advertising

When advertising is switched on, Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads, and those ads may be personalised based on your browsing. You can change this in Google Ads Settings. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent before showing ads.

Children

Pixel Pit is suitable for students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Players should use initials only (for example, "AB") and should not enter a full name or any other personal details.

Your choices

You can clear your browser's stored data at any time. To ask for a high score to be removed, contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
